About us

The YCDI at a glance

The YCDI acts as the youth forum of the IDC-CDI, entitling its member parties to a platform for promoting young leaders on an international scale. As part of the mission of the IDC-CDI, the YCDI objective is to encourage political innovation within a united front of centre political youth organizations adjoined by three key tenets:

  1. 01The defense of democracy against authoritarianism
  2. 02The respect for multiculturalism
  3. 03The empowerment of the political youth
Our core ideas

What we stand for

Five principles guide every statement, project and school of the YCDI, from Brussels to every member party around the world.

01

Defense of Democracy

Democracy is the best avenue for enacting political action; defending it is a moral responsibility of all YCDI members.

02

Strong institutions

Political parties must encourage strong institutions that protect citizens' rights and property from arbitrariness.

03

Generational change

Generational change is key for any political organization. It must be sponsored actively through party statutes.

04

Liberty

Liberty is a necessary condition for any legitimate democratic political process.

05

Good policy-making

Leaders must be good policy makers; member parties of the IDC-CDI must promote the effective leadership of their youth.
